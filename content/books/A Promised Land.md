**Barack Obama** · 2020

The first volume of Obama's presidential memoirs follows his path from early political ambitions to the 2008 campaign and his first term, up to the operation that killed Osama bin Laden in 2011. It covers the financial crisis, health-care reform and foreign policy from the inside.

## Key ideas

- Big decisions come with imperfect information and trade-offs.
- Leadership means balancing ideals against the limits of politics.
- The personal cost of public life on family.
