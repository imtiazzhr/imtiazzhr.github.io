*Quick and Easy Guide to Stock Market Investment for Absolute Beginners*

**AMS Publishing Group** · 2015 · Investing
<span class="book-shelf">Read</span>

This guide, published under the name AMS Publishing Group, is aimed at readers with no experience of the stock market. It presents itself as an introduction to day trading: what it is, how it differs from other trading styles and from long-term investing, and what beginners should realistically expect before risking money.

Based on its description, it covers the practical basics a new trader meets first: choosing tools and a broker, selecting actively traded stocks, limiting the risk taken on each trade and preparing before the market opens. It also stresses discipline and trading psychology, treating trading as a skill built through practice rather than a quick route to wealth. As the title suggests, it is pitched as a quick introduction rather than a comprehensive text.

## Key insights

- Treat trading as a skill that takes time to learn, ideally practised with a simulator or very small sums before real money is at stake.
- Decide in advance how much of your account you are prepared to lose on any single trade, and keep that figure small.
- Short-term trading and long-term investing are different activities. They demand different amounts of time, skill and temperament, so choose deliberately.
- Prepare before the market opens and set entry and exit points in advance, so decisions are not made on impulse in the moment.
- Discipline matters as much as strategy. Breaking your own rules under pressure is a common way for beginners to lose money.

*Read it if you are curious about day trading and want a basic overview before committing money or paying for courses.*
