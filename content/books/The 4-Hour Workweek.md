**Timothy Ferriss** · 2007

Tim Ferriss challenges the idea of working for decades and retiring later. He offers a framework for designing a life with more time, mobility and income now, using elimination, automation and remote work.

## Key ideas

- DEAL: Definition, Elimination, Automation, Liberation.
- Apply the 80/20 rule and Parkinson's law to cut low-value work.
- Take “mini-retirements” spread through life instead of one at the end.
- Automate and outsource to buy back time.
