**Josh Kaufman** · 2010

A self-taught business education in one volume. Kaufman argues that the core ideas of business can be learned without an expensive degree, and lays them out as a set of simple mental models.

## Key ideas

- Every business does five things: creates value, markets it, sells it, delivers it and manages the money.
- Understanding human behaviour matters as much as understanding spreadsheets.
- Systems thinking helps you spot constraints, feedback loops and leverage points.
