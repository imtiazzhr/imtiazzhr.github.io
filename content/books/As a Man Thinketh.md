**James Allen** · 1903

A short classic essay arguing that our thoughts shape our character, our circumstances and our health. Allen encourages readers to take responsibility for their inner life as the starting point for any outward change.

## Key ideas

- Circumstances reveal a person more than they create them.
- Purposeful thinking turns into achievement.
- Calmness is a sign of self-mastery.
