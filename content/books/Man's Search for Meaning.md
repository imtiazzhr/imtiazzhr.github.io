**Viktor E. Frankl** · 1946

Psychiatrist Viktor Frankl recounts his years in Nazi concentration camps and what they taught him about why some people endure unbearable suffering. The second half introduces logotherapy, his approach built on the idea that the search for meaning is our strongest drive.

## Key ideas

- Everything can be taken from a person except the freedom to choose their attitude.
- Meaning can be found through work, through love, and in how we face unavoidable suffering.
- People who have a “why” to live can bear almost any “how”.
