*Tesla, SpaceX, and the Quest for a Fantastic Future*

**Ashlee Vance** · 2015 · Biography
<span class="book-shelf">Read</span>

Technology journalist Ashlee Vance traces Elon Musk's life from a difficult, bookish childhood in South Africa to his move to North America, his early internet companies Zip2 and PayPal, and his decision to put his fortune into SpaceX, Tesla and SolarCity. Drawing on long interviews with Musk and conversations with family, friends, former employees and critics, Vance describes how close SpaceX and Tesla came to collapse in 2008.

The book presents Musk as an engineer-entrepreneur driven by vast goals, such as making humanity multi-planetary and speeding the shift to sustainable energy, and as a demanding, often harsh manager. Because it was published in 2015, it covers his rise and early successes rather than his later ventures and controversies.

## Key insights

- Reason from first principles; Musk costed a rocket by its raw materials rather than by market prices, revealing large room for savings.
- Making key parts in-house can speed up iteration and cut costs when suppliers are slow, expensive or set in their ways.
- A bold mission attracts talented people willing to endure hardship; many engineers joined for the goal as much as for the pay.
- Extreme deadlines can push teams to surprising results, but the book also records the burnout and personal costs they caused.
- Persistence through near-failure matters; SpaceX's Falcon 1 rocket reached orbit only on its fourth attempt, with money running out.

*Read it if you want to understand how SpaceX and Tesla were built, and the methods and costs behind Musk's early success.*
