**Matthew McConaughey** · 2020

Drawing on decades of journals, the actor tells stories from his upbringing, his travels and his career. A “greenlight” is a go-ahead; the book is about spotting them, and about turning life's red and yellow lights into green ones over time.

## Key ideas

- Setbacks often turn into greenlights with time and perspective.
- Knowing what you won't do is as useful as knowing what you will.
- Keeping a journal lets you learn from your own life.
