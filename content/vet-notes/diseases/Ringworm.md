title: "Ringworm"
date: 2020-10-11
aliases:
  - "Dermatophytosis"
---

- Ringworm is common in young stock and is readily transmitted to humans (zoonosis). The greyish lesions are slightly raised, well-circumscribed, and are more common on the head and neck but may extend over much of the body. **Fig 3: Ringworm lesions are greyish, well-circumscribed, and are more common on the head and neck (ear in this image).**
