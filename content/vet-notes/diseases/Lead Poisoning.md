### Clinical signs

- Weakness & lethargy
- Loss of weight
- Loss of appetite
- Digestive symptoms
  - Delay in food digestion
  - Diarrhoea
  - Food regurgitation
  - Dark coloured faeces
- Neurological symptoms
  - Irregular falcon movements
  - Tremor’s and irregular head movements
  - Deviation of the head to one side
  - The falcon walks in a circular path
  - Slouching wings
  - Blindness
  - Convulsions and muscular twitches
  - Leg and / or wing paralysis (severe cases)

### Diagnosis and differentials

- History, xrays

### Treatment and control

- depends upon the severity and position of lead. surgery , stomach lavage

### Summary

- Can be acute or chronic. Usually by hunting pallets, or feeding birds or rodents to falcons
