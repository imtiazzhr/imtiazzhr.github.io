---
title: "Normal parameters"
date: 2020-10-11
---

- Camel:       Temp 37.5–38.9°C         HR   60–90 beats/min      RR   10–30 breaths/min
- Horse:        Temp 37 –38.5°C          HR   36–44  beats/min      RR   8–15 breaths/min
- Newborn foals’ heart rates range from 80 to 120, older foals from 60 to 80, and yearlings from 40 to 60 beats per minute.
- Dogs:         Temp 38 –39.2°C          HR   80–120 beats/min     RR   15–30 breaths/min
- Cats:          Temp 38 –39.2°C          HR   100–140 beats/min    RR   20–30 breaths/min
- Sheep:       Temp 38.5 –40°C          HR   70–90 beats/min         RR   20–30 breaths/min
- Goat:         Temp 39 –40°C              HR   70–100 beats/min       RR   15–30 breaths/min
- Cattle:        Temp 38 –39  °C           HR   60–80 beats/min          RR   15–30 breaths/min
