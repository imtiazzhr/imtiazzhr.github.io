- As we start to prepare for new arrivals to make their way into the world let’s consider the important milestones in a foal’s first 24 hours of life
- * Sitting up within 5 minutes from birth
- * Starting to attempt to stand within 30 minutes and successfully standing within 2 hours from birth
- * Nursing within 3 hours from birth
- * First faeces (meconium) passed within 8 hours from birth
- * First urine passed 6-8 hours from birth (sometimes longer for fillies)
- * Sitting up within 5 minutes from birth
- * Nursing within 3 hours from birth
