## Professional Summary

Veterinarian with a decade of clinical experience in equine and camel practice, with a focus on sports medicine, lameness, diagnostic imaging, surgery and reproduction. Since 2018 my clinical work has centred on dromedary camels, serving race, breeding and show animals in hospital and ambulatory settings in Qatar and the UAE, and I have helped set up ambulatory services from scratch at two practices. I have also worked at clinics in Oman and, earlier, with Brooke in Pakistan on working-equine health and field-vet training. I hold a diploma in business development, growth and digital marketing from the Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania, and I am building VetMor, a veterinary web application that applies clinical insight to digital tools.
