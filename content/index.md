Salaam, I'm Imtiaz.

I'm a veterinarian in Abu Dhabi. For about ten years I've worked with horses and camels in Pakistan, Qatar, Oman, Saudi Arabia and the UAE, mostly in sports medicine, lameness and diagnostic imaging. On the side I build things: a vet company, some software, and a planning system that stops me from trying to do all of it at once.
