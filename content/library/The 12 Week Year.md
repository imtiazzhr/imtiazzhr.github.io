**Brian P. Moran and Michael Lennington** · 2013

The authors argue that yearly goals give us too much time: urgency only arrives in November, so most of the year is wasted. Their answer is to treat twelve weeks as a full “year”, with its own goals, a weekly plan and a weekly score of how much of the plan you actually carried out.

## Key ideas

- A shorter horizon creates urgency and focus.
- Plan every week from your 12-week goals, and score your execution; around 85% is a strong week.
- Track the actions you control (lead indicators), not only the results you hope for.
- Use a thirteenth week to review, rest and plan the next cycle.
