**Yuval Noah Harari** · 2016

Having largely brought famine, plague and war under control, Harari suggests, humanity will next pursue immortality, happiness and god-like powers. He explores how biotechnology and algorithms may reshape work, politics and what it means to be human.

## Key ideas

- Humanism put human feelings at the centre of meaning; data may displace it (“dataism”).
- Algorithms may soon know us better than we know ourselves.
- Many people risk becoming economically irrelevant as intelligence decouples from consciousness.
