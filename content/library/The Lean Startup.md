**Eric Ries** · 2011

Eric Ries treats a startup as an experiment run under extreme uncertainty. Instead of long plans and big launches, he proposes building the smallest thing that can test an idea, measuring how real customers respond, and learning fast.

## Key ideas

- Build, measure, learn: shorten the loop as much as you can.
- A minimum viable product exists to learn, not to impress.
- Measure progress by validated learning, not by activity.
- Decide deliberately whether to pivot or persevere.
