**Jonas Salzgeber** · 2019

A practical introduction to Stoic philosophy for modern life. Salzgeber explains the core ideas of the Stoics and turns them into everyday exercises for dealing with stress, anger and setbacks.

## Key ideas

- Focus on what is within your control and accept what isn't.
- Virtue, not circumstance, is the foundation of a good life.
- Imagining losses in advance helps you appreciate what you have.
- Small daily practices build resilience over time.
