I'm Imtiaz Zahoor, a veterinarian. I was born and raised in central Pakistan and earned my Doctor of Veterinary Medicine at the University of Agriculture, Faisalabad, in 2016. My full CV is [[cv|here]].

## In the clinic

I started as a junior ambulatory vet with The Brooke, looking after working horses, donkeys and mules. In 2018 I moved to Qatar to join the sports medicine and surgery team at Tharb Camel Hospital & Research Center, where I worked alongside board-certified surgeons from Europe and the US. After that I worked at Nawames Equine & Camel Clinic and ran my own ambulatory practice for race, breeding and show camels.

Today I'm a clinician at a hospital in Abu Dhabi, treating horses, camels and wildlife.

Along the way I spent time at equine and large-animal practices in Belgium, Germany, the Netherlands and the US, to see how other people do the job.

The parts of the work I care most about are sports medicine and lameness, diagnostic imaging, regenerative medicine, and assisted reproduction in camels.

## Outside the clinic

I co-founded Vetmor in 2020 to bring more technology into animal healthcare, and these days I'm building software for veterinary practices. I read a lot, mostly about business and ideas, and I follow the markets more closely than is probably healthy. When I can, I travel, camp, fly a drone, play chess and write code.

I'm fuelled by coffee, curiosity and a love for deep conversations.
