**Imtiaz Zahoor, DVM**
Veterinary clinician · Abu Dhabi, UAE · [imtiazdvm@gmail.com](mailto:imtiazdvm@gmail.com)

## Profile

Veterinary clinician with about ten years of experience in equine and camel practice in the Middle East and Pakistan. My focus is sports medicine and lameness, diagnostic imaging, regenerative medicine and assisted reproduction in camels. I have worked in hospital and ambulatory practice, trained veterinary assistants and newly hired vets, and helped build veterinary services from the ground up.

## Experience

**Veterinary clinician** · Equine hospital, Abu Dhabi, UAE · 2024 – present
- Clinical work with horses, camels and wildlife, with a focus on sports medicine, lameness and diagnostic imaging.

**Veterinarian** · Nawames Equine & Camel Clinic and own ambulatory practice, Qatar · 2021 – 2024
- Ambulatory services for race, breeding and show camels: lameness and performance work-ups, diagnostic imaging, reproduction and herd health.

**Associate Veterinarian, Sports Medicine and Surgery** · Tharb Camel Hospital & Research Center, Qatar · 2018 – 2021
- Worked alongside ECVS and ACVS diplomate surgeons in sports medicine and surgery.
- Helped revamp and expand the hospital's services, including its ambulatory service.
- Part-time work at the government racecourse and small-animal clinics in Doha.

**Junior Veterinarian** · The Brooke Hospital for Working Equines, Pakistan · 2016 – 2018
- Ambulatory care for working horses, donkeys and mules, after my internship.
